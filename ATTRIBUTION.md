## ⚠️ Before using this commercially

The Commons photographs are **CC BY-SA**. That licence requires you to:

1. **Credit** the author and name the licence wherever the image appears, and
2. **Share alike** — release your adapted version under the same licence.

Cropping and resizing (which this build does) counts as adapting. For a real client
site you will usually want to swap those fifteen files for images you have licensed
outright, or for Unsplash/Pexels photos which carry no such condition. The Unsplash
images and the original artwork above have no attribution or share-alike obligation.
